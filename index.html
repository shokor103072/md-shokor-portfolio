Biography

Md Shokor A Rahaman is a PhD researcher at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and engineering. His work focuses on research that can move from experimentation into useful, reliable systems.

His research interests span deep learning, explainable AI, computer vision, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, distributed acoustic sensing, and energy intelligence. Across these areas, he investigates models that are accurate, interpretable, and practical under real-world constraints.

His work in data-driven engineering includes predictive modelling and optimization for reservoir characterization, total organic carbon estimation, well placement, material properties, and geological carbon storage.

He is also interested in efficient intelligent systems: compact models for edge deployment, robust learning from complex sensor signals, and structured AI workflows that combine retrieval, reasoning, and automation.

Through research and engineering, he aims to build transparent and dependable AI systems with practical value across energy, sensing, healthcare, and scientific applications.
