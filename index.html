Biography

Md Shokor A Rahaman is a PhD researcher in Information Technology at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS. His research focuses on reliable AI for distributed acoustic sensing, microseismic analysis, and scientific decision support.

His publications span microseismic classification, reservoir characterization, optimization, hydropower, and machine learning for engineering systems. He has also built language and document AI systems in research and industry roles.

Since June 2026, he has served as Chief Technology Officer at T3 IT Solutions and Al Hamra Group of Companies.

At UTP, he serves in student leadership across the Association of International Students and the Post Graduate Student Council. View leadership roles or read the CV.

His research service includes 55 reviews recorded on ORCID as of September 2026: 50 for Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence and 5 for Array. He also plays cricket, football, and table tennis. See service and activities.
